Overlord_exe Help

Overlord_exe manages Tartarus lock contracts over Bluesky DMs using composable rules, keyholder proxy controls, and optional public Bluesky announcements.

You can talk to Overlord_exe in plain English via Bluesky DM, or use explicit !commands.

1. Self-Locking & Composable Rules

Ask Overlord to lock you (e.g. "Lock me for a few hours" or "Lock me for Bluesky tasks") or send !timer, !superbot, or !lockme. You'll receive a magic link to configure your session and combine any of the following rules:

  • Timer — Lock for a fixed duration or a randomized range (the exact end time is revealed after you accept).
  • Bluesky Likes Goal — Accumulate a target number of likes on your Bluesky image posts to unlock.
  • Bluesky Crowd Time — Overlord posts a public notice; community likes and reposts during the open window add time to your lock.
  • Release Policies — Require All selected rules to complete, Any single rule to complete, or Manual keyholder release.
  • Public Announcements — Check Announce publicly to have the Announcer bot post to Bluesky when your contract is accepted and released.

2. Keyholder Proxy Control

Lock and manage another Bluesky user's device without sharing raw control outside Bluesky:

  • Issue a proxy contract: Say "Lock @user.bsky.social" (or "Publicly lock @user.bsky.social"), or use !own @user (as keyholder) / !submit @keyholder (as sub).
  • Control an active contract: Say "Unlock @user", "Relock @user", or "Release @user", or use !unlock @user, !lock @user, and !release @user.

3. Shareable Tokens & User Profile

Overlord remembers your shareable tokens (s-... and tc-...) automatically whenever you mention them in a DM or submit a form, and auto-injects them into future self-locks and proxy contracts.

  • !token <token> — Save a shareable token and set it as your default.
  • !tokens — List all shareable tokens currently saved on your profile.

4. Emergency Relock

If you trigger an emergency unlock on your device and want to resume your active contract(s), say "Relock me" or send !relock.

5. Bluesky Community Lists

Ask to join or leave a list (e.g. "Add me to Subs"), or use !add <list> / !remove <list>. Available lists: Doms, Subs, and Public Property.
